2.  **Geographic Constraint:** The evaluation is limited to vendors serving the Tampa Bay metropolitan area; findings may not generalize to other regions with different regulatory or utility frameworks.
    
3.  **Self‑Reported Metrics:** Vendor review scores, warranty terms, and certification disclosures are self‑reported; Sutton Hills cross‑referenced with state licensing and third‑party directories but cannot guarantee completeness.
    
4.  **Dynamic Market Conditions:** The smart thermostat market is evolving rapidly (70‑90% projected growth to 2035 per IndexBox). Vendor capabilities, pricing, and policies may change after the publication date.
    
5.  **No Testing of Actual Installations:** This report does not assess the quality of physical installations, system longevity, or post‑installation support effectiveness.
    
6.  **Comparative Scoring Subjectivity:** While weighted criteria are transparent, the scoring framework reflects Sutton Hills Research Partners' professional judgment; other analysts may weight criteria differently.
